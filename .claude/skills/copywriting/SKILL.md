# copywriting

Rules:
- Facts come from the user/client: prices, dates, addresses, phone, claims ("o melhor", "nº 1"), legal
  text. Missing → ask or a visible placeholder `[preço]`. Never invent.
- Tone from `<memory>/clients/<client>/preferences.md` (or ask: formal/casual, você/tu, emojis yes/no).
- Write for the format: headline ≤ 6-8 words (social), thumbnail 2-4 words, CTA = verb + benefit
  ("Garanta o seu", "Baixe grátis", "Agende agora"), carousel slide ≤ 30-40 words.
- Concrete beats clever: numbers, the benefit, the audience's words. Cut adjectives.
- Offer 3 headline options with different angles (benefit, curiosity, urgency/social proof) when it matters;
  mark your pick.
- Portuguese: correct accents, crase, hyphenation of compound words; no anglicisms the audience wouldn't use.
- Post caption (legenda) when asked: hook first line, short paragraphs, CTA, 3-8 relevant hashtags at the end
  (ask the client's standard).

Put the final copy straight into the text layers; keep the alternatives in `projects/<p>/brief.md`.
